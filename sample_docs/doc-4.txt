Modern databases rely on balanced trees, hash tables, skip lists, and bitmap indexes to achieve fast lookup performance. Search engines combine inverted indexes with positional data, term frequency counts, document frequency statistics, and relevance ranking algorithms such as cosine similarity, BM25, and vector space scoring. Distributed systems introduce sharding, replication, caching, load balancing, fault tolerance, and consistency protocols to scale retrieval across clusters, servers, nodes, partitions, and geographically separated data centers.
